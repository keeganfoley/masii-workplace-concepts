INTERACTIVE / YOUR LAUNCH FORMAT
The starting point

Lunch + guided signup

Our team visits the office, brings lunch, demonstrates the app and helps employees sign up and make their first Play.

  1. Set up the company

    HR confirms staff, approved causes and the launch date.

  2. Invite & verify

    Open the company link or scan its QR code. Verify eligibility.

  3. Activate & choose

    Claim employer-paid access. Pick a cause. No card needed.

  4. Make a first play

    Complete a daily action. See your GoodPrint progress.

  5. Help them return

    Get a next-day prompt, a first-week recap and help as needed.

Optional: a same-day company Drop — a prize drawing with approved rules, a free-entry route and agreed funding.
